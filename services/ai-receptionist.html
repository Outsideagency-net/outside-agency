The Problem

You Paid For the Click. Then Nobody Answered.

Most local service businesses lose leads at the phone. Calls come in while the team is on a job, after hours, or on a busy line. The caller does not leave a voicemail. They call the next company on the list. Every missed call is ad spend that never had a chance to pay off.

What It Does

A Receptionist That Never Misses a Call.

Answers Every Call

A natural sounding voice picks up right away, day or night, so callers reach a real conversation instead of a voicemail box.

Books Appointments

It checks your availability, offers times, and puts the appointment on your calendar while the caller is still on the line.

Routes What Matters

Emergencies and high value calls can be sent straight to your team, while routine questions are handled for you.

Built Around Your Business

Your services, service area, hours, and way of speaking are set up for your company, not a generic script.

How It Works

From Setup to Live Calls.

1. We learn your services, hours, and booking rules
2. We configure the voice agent and connect your calendar
3. We set routing rules for emergencies and transfers
4. We test it with real call scenarios before launch
5. You go live and review call outcomes with us
6. We refine the script and rules as calls come in
Who It's For

Built for Local Service Businesses.

Home services, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, beauty, and other appointment driven businesses where the phone is the main way customers reach you. It works best alongside paid search and Local Services Ads, since those channels send high intent callers who expect an answer.

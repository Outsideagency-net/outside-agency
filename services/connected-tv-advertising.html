The Opportunity

Traditional TV Wastes Money on People You Will Never Serve.

A local business advertising on broadcast or cable pays to reach an entire market, including viewers far outside its service area. Meanwhile, more and more viewing has moved to streaming apps and smart TVs. Connected TV puts your video on that same big screen, but you choose where it runs and who sees it.

Why It Works Locally

Highly Local. Highly Targeted.

Hyper Local Reach

Target by city, county, ZIP code, or a radius around your location, so your budget is spent on households inside your service area.

Audience Targeting

Layer in homeowner status, household characteristics, interests, and in-market signals to reach the people most likely to need what you sell.

Big Screen Impact

Full screen video on the living room TV gets attention that a small banner ad rarely can. It is the biggest screen your brand can show up on.

Reach Cord Cutters

Reach households that no longer watch cable or broadcast, through the streaming apps and services they use every day.

Built for Local Businesses

Local Reach Without a Local TV Budget.

Home services, HVAC, beauty, and other local businesses have always been priced out of TV. Connected TV changes that. You can start with a tightly defined service area, learn what works, and expand from there. Pair it with paid search and social to stay in front of the same households across every screen.
